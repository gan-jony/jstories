Jachnun Stories is no longer taking orders. This page stays online as a keepsake of a very tasty chapter.

The story of rolling

It started as an underground tradition: Jachnun brunch for friends. Then it became a weekly one, thanks to the feedback of the people who came back for more. The rolling and the baking were mine. The support and demand were what let Jachnun Stories grow.

I run a tech startup, and when the pandemic slowed everything down, my calendar suddenly had free weekends. I skipped another Netflix binge, pulled out my Yemenite grandma's original Jachnun recipe and my grandpa's secret pepper schug, and started to roll. After years of friendly, sarcastic "what about my Jachnun?" from my local community, I finally gave it a whole weekend. Then the next one. Then another.

Delicious, authentic, handmade with love. Simply enjoy it.

What it was

Original Jachnun, Kubaneh bread and a vegan Jachnun, all homemade to order, baked overnight and delivered across London on weekend mornings. Served with a baked egg, grated tomato and a spicy schug made from my grandfather's recipe.

Thank you to everyone who ordered, shared, and rolled with me.
